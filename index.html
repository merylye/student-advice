Advice for Students

I curated this site primarily for early PhD students and undergrads interested in research.

There is a ton of written advice, resources, and so on out on the internet. These are my favorites, accompanied by my own musings, advice I've collected from practicums and seminars, and a library of links you can filter by topic, stage, and field.

tl;drMake sure you enjoy what you do. High quality research takes time, and it's okay that it does!
