- **Good mentors make all the difference.** A good mentor can foster a passion, and a bad mentor can kill an interest. Build your team of mentors deliberately, and when you're ready, become one for someone younger than you.
- **Don't clout chase** or pursue projects with a mindset of doing it before someone else does. High quality research takes time. Aim for work that would still be insightful decades from now.
- **Talk to people.** While search engines and AI are useful for brainstorming and querying knowledge, field experts offer independent, nuanced perspectives you won't find elsewhere. Research involves much more socializing than most of us anticipated, and meeting new people may turn out to be the most enjoyable part.
- **Impact starts local.** Big-picture societal impact is hard to see or control, and thinking too far ahead gets stressful. Focus on what you can do for your local community and bubble, and the gratification is much more immediate. Let your interests and passions guide the rest.
- **Enjoy the process.** It's where you spend most of your time.
